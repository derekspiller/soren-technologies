About

Built for disciplined operations

Soren Technologies is an aerospace and defense company focused on the intersection of cyber, intelligence, electromagnetic warfare, and communications.

We prioritize clarity, restraint, and operator utility—software that organizes complex RF and security work, and hardware that supports controlled, intentional use in training and operational contexts.

  • Electromagnetic warfare Spectrum-centric tooling and emitters for awareness and controlled presence.

  • Communications Antennas and mesh radio options for resilient connectivity.
